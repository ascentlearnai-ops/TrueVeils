WHAT TRUVEIL LOOKS FOR

Possible AI assistance.
Evidence, not assumptions.

Truveil is built for technical interview integrity. It combines several kinds of context so reviewers can ask better follow-up questions.

Read the detection limits
  1. 01

    Restricted-tool activity

    Known AI apps and destinations are matched against the session policy. Allowed tools are treated differently from restricted ones.

  2. 02

    Transcript and timing signals

    Assistant-like artifacts, response timing, and changes within the conversation can add review context. Polished language alone is not proof.

  3. 03

    A report you can question

    Review bands, timestamps, notes, and counter-evidence stay together. Missing or low-quality evidence can remain inconclusive.

THE THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Ask us something else
Can Truveil prove that someone used AI?

No. Truveil helps identify possible AI assistance and policy events for human review. Its expert-configured signals are not statistically calibrated probabilities, may miss activity, and can be inconclusive. Never use a Truveil signal as the sole basis for a hiring decision.

What does the candidate need to install?

Truveil Secure for Windows. The candidate receives an invitation code, reviews the session policy, and consents before joining.

Can our team try it yet?

Join the waitlist to tell us about your team. We will contact you about availability and onboarding. Existing beta participants can continue using the Windows apps.

Does it work with our video meeting app?

Truveil runs alongside your meeting. Recruiters can configure allowed apps and websites, including common tools such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet. Desktop monitoring is best effort.
