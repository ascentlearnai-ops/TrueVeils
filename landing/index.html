Privacy, in plain language

What Truveil doesn't do.

No video recording

No camera feed and no screen recording. Truveil stores timestamped events, not a film of the candidate.

No biometric analysis

No eye tracking, emotion detection, facial scoring, or claims about what a person feels.

No automated pass or fail

Advisory evidence supports a human review. Truveil never makes the hiring decision.

Consent is collected in-app before any session begins. Candidates can end a session at any time.

FAQ

Questions careful teams ask first.

What does the candidate see?+

A clear consent notice, session-code field, microphone health, connection status, and restricted-site policy. They never see a risk score.

What data is stored?+

The review record can include transcript text, timestamped session events, sources, notes, and follow-up prompts. Raw fallback audio is deleted after transcription.

Does Truveil work on Mac?+

The paid beta is Windows only. We would rather state that plainly than promise a platform we are not ready to support.

How do you handle false positives?+

Truveil does not produce an accusation or automatic rejection. Every signal remains attached to context and requires human review.

Does Truveil make hiring decisions?+

No. It produces a reviewable record. The hiring team decides what the evidence means.

How stable is the beta?+

Core reviewer workflows are stable. Small issues can still appear, and support is visible in both apps and on this site.
